# Data

The ROGII competition data is not included in this repository.

To access it, join the Kaggle competition and accept its rules on the official
[competition page](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rogii-wellbore-geology-prediction).
Follow Kaggle's current download instructions and keep all files under a local
`data/` directory, which is ignored by Git.

This repository does not redistribute:

- raw train/test archives;
- derived labels, OOF predictions or feature caches;
- sample or final submission files;
- trained model weights.

Use `python examples/synthetic_demo.py` to exercise the published mechanisms
without any competition data.
