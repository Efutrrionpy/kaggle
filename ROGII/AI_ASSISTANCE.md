# AI assistance disclosure

This project used AI agents as engineering and research tools.

- The project owner defined the objective, validation standard, experiment
  priorities, model-selection rules and final accountability.
- Codex agents implemented and executed a substantial portion of the code,
  tests, experiment orchestration and Kaggle release operations.
- Claude Opus was used selectively for independent research criticism,
  counterarguments and blind-spot discovery, not as experimental evidence.

The public case study is intentionally explicit about this collaboration. It
does not claim that every source file or experiment was manually authored by
one person.
