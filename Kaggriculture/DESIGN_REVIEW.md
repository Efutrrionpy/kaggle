# Transferable research lessons

The final Silver result missed the Gold target. That does not make every
rejected experiment a mistake, nor does a successful leading solution prove
that copying its method earlier would have won. These lessons concern which
decisions our actual evidence could support.

## 1. Test control capacity before judging a method family

The August 23 balanced BC correction achieved 99.71% held-out row accuracy and
nearly reproduced its teacher's rollout money. Both still lost 0–8 to the
mirror opponent: six SELL heads could not repair fixed worker logistics.
Rejecting more optimization of that wrapper was reasonable. The unresolved
decision was whether to fund a genuinely full-action formulation when better
demonstrations became available. [Measured record](results/early_research_decisions.json).

The mechanism is a mismatch between what training can change and what causes
the outcome. Before extending or closing a family, identify its effective
control variables and test the teacher/representation ceiling. Treat a good
optimizer inside a weak interface as a redesign signal, not evidence against
learning generally. This applies when action scope, decoder or representation
can impose a ceiling; it does not require trying every conceivable model.

## 2. Fresh test units do not make an old comparison population representative

Committed gained 11 wins over FlexService across 896 cells, with no regressions,
but all 11 were against LateFlock; the other six opponents stayed 733/768 for
both. The gain was real, while its relevance to the final field was narrow.
The large September 28 replay inventory likewise did not establish recent executable
opponent coverage. [Results](RESULTS.md), [grouped evidence](results/committed_funding_confirmation_20260930_groups.json).

Track who and what an experiment represents, not just whether its seeds are
unused. Compare remaining errors with the current deployment population and
seek a discriminating new stratum when the panel is saturated. In a dynamic
game this means opponent behavior and time; elsewhere it may mean site, device
or acquisition regime. New data is not automatically independent. Unavailable
opponents remain a stated coverage gap, not a reason to treat tapes as reactive.

## 3. Diagnose the complete causal chain, then remeasure the actual objective

NativeDeadlines gained 18 net outcomes but introduced two losses in one world.
Extra feeding at turn 115 removed a turn 120 wheat sale; the forecast ended at
143, missing a turn 168 hire that required 33 cash when only 32 remained.
Extending the funding horizon repaired that measured mechanism. CareTrade later
looked useful in development but added no prospective wins over Committed.
[Causal evidence](results/native_deadlines_confirmation_20260930_regression_mechanism.json),
[fresh results](results/care_trade_confirmation_20260930_groups.json).

An intermediate feasibility check or attractive margin is not the deployment
objective. Trace effects through the next binding commitment and compare the
complete outcome under the real selection rule. This applies to sequential
systems and coupled components; it does not justify an unlimited simulator
when a small counterexample already separates hypotheses. Our latest-two,
best-agent tournament was also not a hindsight per-game best-of-two selector.
Keeping that distinction was necessary.

## 4. Verify that a treatment changes behavior before scaling evaluation

The conservative router consumed 1,232 games and then 1,744 more after expanded
calibration, yet every gate stayed disabled and paired outcomes remained
identical. More calibration was a plausible correction, but unchanged activation
explained why another large outcome comparison could not demonstrate an active
policy improvement. [Router evidence](results/early_research_decisions.json).

Check reachability on existing development states before paying for a large new
panel. If the treatment is inactive, determine whether calibration, data or
representation—not outcome evaluation—is the unresolved question. Activation
is not a promotion criterion and must never be tuned on a consumed holdout
merely to manufacture a gain. This applies to abstaining models, routers,
rare-event interventions and optional pipeline stages.

## 5. Keep exact deployment checks and narrow claims to their scope

Both final archives reproduced 1,438 actions across the two seats of their
official verification episodes and rebuild byte-for-byte. This checked the
submitted entry point, not a convenient surrogate. Conversely, high local win
rates did not certify Gold. At closeout, the legacy monitor's `MEDAL_CONFIRMED`
label covered Silver as well as Gold; reading the actual medal prevented an
incorrect success declaration. [Deployment evidence](results/deployment_checks.json),
[final result](results/final_result_20261022.json).

Bind expensive checks to concrete failure risks and retain their results so
they need not be repeated without a relevant change. Separately verify the
target predicate after an objective update. This is not weaker validation:
execution parity, data validity, representative evaluation and official success
are different claims and require different evidence.

## Limits of the retrospective

Hardware failures, consultation-delivery defects and some lost temporary
prototype closures were real infrastructure/reproducibility constraints. A
better prompt alone would not fix them. Nor can we identify how much final
rank was caused by any one choice. Available leading accounts make full-action
learning credible; they do not supply a randomized counterfactual for this
project. The supported improvement is to make formulation, coverage and
downstream-effect questions explicit earlier, while retaining reproducibility
and honest negative results.
