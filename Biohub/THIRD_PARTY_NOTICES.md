# Biohub Sources and Licenses

| Component | Source and attribution |
|---|---|
| Temporal tracking baseline | [nusrati/0-940](https://www.kaggle.com/code/nusrati/0-940); full notebook redistribution terms were not established |
| Threshold evidence | [anvithpothula/biohub-x52](https://www.kaggle.com/code/anvithpothula/biohub-x52) |
| Neural checkpoints | Referenced pilkwang datasets; retained metadata declared CC0-1.0 |

| Published motion code, synthetic tests, and report text | Original project material under the existing [MIT license](LICENSE) |

The model dataset declarations do not establish redistribution rights for
other authors' notebooks. This repository supplies the original motion
implementation and checkpoint references, rather than embedding the external
notebook source, weights, or wheelhouse.

NumPy, SciPy, and Polars are installed separately under their own licenses.
Competition images and annotations must be obtained from Kaggle under the
[competition rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/biohub-cell-tracking-during-development/rules).
Leading-solution comparisons in the research report are our summaries with
direct links to the authors' writeups.
